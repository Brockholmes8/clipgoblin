# Clip permission DM templates

Send these through the streamer's business email (usually in their Twitch/YouTube "About" section) or their Discord, as well as a DM. Managers and editors read email more reliably than DMs.

Save every "yes" as a screenshot in a `permissions/` folder with the date. If they ever change their mind, that's your proof.

---

## Short DM (Twitch, X, Instagram, TikTok)

> Hey [NAME]! I run ClipGoblin, a Shorts/TikTok clip page. I'd love to post highlights from your streams as vertical clips with captions, always credited to you with your handle and a link to your channel. Would you be OK with that? Happy to follow any rules you have (which platforms, what to tag, what not to post). Thanks!

## Longer email (business contact or manager)

> **Subject:** Permission to clip your streams for Shorts/TikTok/Reels
>
> Hi [NAME / TEAM],
>
> I'm [YOUR NAME] and I run ClipGoblin ([LINK]). I make vertical highlight clips with captions and would like to post moments from [STREAMER]'s streams on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
>
> What I'd do:
> - Credit [STREAMER] in every video (on-screen handle plus a link in the caption)
> - Use only public VODs or footage you provide
> - Take down any clip you ask me to, right away
> - Follow any rules you have on topics, sponsors, or platforms
>
> If you run a clipping program or have clipping rules I should follow, I'd be glad to join.
>
> Could you confirm by replying to this email whether that's OK?
>
> Thanks,
> [YOUR NAME]

## What to look for in the reply

- A clear "yes" in writing, not just a like or emoji.
- Any conditions, such as platforms, credit format, no monetization, or required hashtags. Put them in the `--credit` text and follow them.
- If they say "join our clipping program", do that instead. It pays.
